## 1. Who we are and who this policy covers

Roam Caller sells travel eSIMs: digital SIM profiles that give your phone mobile data in more than 108 countries. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use our website, buy or top up an eSIM, or contact us, and what we do with it.

**Who is responsible.** Internova Technologies Limited, 431 Woodcrest Dr SE, Washington, DC 20032, USA, which owns the Roam Caller brand ("Roam Caller", "we", "us") is the data controller for customers who buy from us directly.

**If you bought through one of our business partners** (for example a bank, fintech app, airline, travel agency or insurer), that partner decides how your data is used in their product and is the controller for it. We process the data needed to issue and run your eSIM on the partner's behalf, under a written agreement with them. Their privacy notice applies to everything they do; this policy applies to what we do.

## 2. What we collect

We collect only what we need to sell, deliver and support your eSIM. **We never see the content of your browsing, messages or calls**: your traffic passes through the mobile networks abroad, not through our systems.

| Type | Examples | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity and contact | Name, email address, phone number, country of residence | You, or the business partner you bought through |
| Order details | Plans bought, prices, currency, dates, top-ups, refunds, the partner's order reference | Our systems |
| Payment | Payment method type, the last 4 digits of a card, transaction references. We never store full card numbers or bank PINs. | Our payment providers |
| eSIM and device | eSIM identifier (ICCID), installation status, phone make and model, operating system version | Our eSIM supplier and your device |
| Usage and network | Data used and remaining, expiry date, the country and mobile network the eSIM connects to, connection times | Our eSIM supplier and its partner networks |
| Identity documents, only where required | Passport or national ID details, when a destination country's law requires checks before an eSIM works there | You, collected by us or our eSIM supplier |
| Support | Messages, call notes, screenshots you send us | You |
| Website and marketing | IP address, browser type, pages viewed, marketing preferences | Your browser and you |

We do not knowingly collect sensitive data such as health, religion or biometrics. The usage and network data above shows roughly where your phone is (the country and network), not your precise location.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

## 5. International transfers

A travel eSIM works across borders, so your data does too. It may be processed in Nigeria, in the countries where our eSIM supplier, payment providers and cloud hosts operate, and in the countries you travel to.

When we send personal data out of the country it was collected in, we use the safeguards that country's law requires. These include:

- transfers to countries the regulator recognises as giving adequate protection
- written contracts with standard data protection clauses, such as those approved by Nigeria's Data Protection Commission (NDPC), the European Commission or the UK
- your consent, or the transfer being necessary to perform our contract with you, for example connecting your eSIM to a network abroad

You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards that apply to your data.

## 7. Security and breaches

We protect personal data with encryption in transit and at rest, access limited to staff who need it, two-factor login for our systems, and regular security reviews. Payment card details are handled by PCI DSS-certified payment providers and never reach our servers.

Treat your eSIM QR code like a key: anyone who scans it before you can install your eSIM. Don't share it, and contact us at once if you think someone else has it.

If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will notify the relevant regulator within the legal deadline (72 hours in Nigeria, Kenya, Uganda and under the GDPR; "as soon as reasonably possible" in South Africa). We will also tell you without undue delay, with what happened and what you can do.

## 8. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

- give you a copy of your personal data
- correct data that is wrong or incomplete
- delete your data, unless we must keep it by law (for example tax records)
- stop or limit a use of your data, including any use based on our legitimate interests
- send your data to you or another provider in a common digital format, where the law provides this
- withdraw your consent, for example to marketing, without affecting what we did before

Write to us at the address in section 9. We may ask you to prove who you are. We answer within 30 days, or sooner where your country's law requires, and we don't charge unless a request is clearly excessive. If you bought through a business partner, we may pass your request to them.
