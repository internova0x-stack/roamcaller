## 2. The service

A Roam Caller eSIM is a digital SIM profile that gives your phone **mobile data** in the countries its plan covers. Each plan states its coverage, data allowance and validity before you buy.

- **Data only.** Unless a plan says otherwise, eSIMs include no phone number, voice calls or SMS. Internet calls and messages (for example WhatsApp) use your data allowance. Keep your home SIM switched on if you need calls, texts or bank OTPs.
- **Partner networks.** Your eSIM connects through mobile networks in each destination, arranged through our eSIM supplier. We do not run those networks.
- **Coverage and speed.** Speeds (3G, 4G or 5G) and coverage depend on the local network, your location, your phone and how busy the network is. We don't guarantee a speed or coverage everywhere, especially indoors, underground, at sea, in the air or in remote areas.
- **Network management.** Local networks may slow certain traffic or block services that are illegal in their country. Some services may be restricted in some destinations.
- **Hotspot.** You may share your data with your own devices unless a plan says otherwise.

## 5. Delivery, installation, validity and top-ups

**Delivery.** We send your eSIM's QR code and install details by email, usually within minutes of payment. If it hasn't arrived within 24 hours, contact us.

**Installation.**

- Most eSIMs can be installed **only once**. If you delete the eSIM from your phone, it usually can't be reinstalled and the remaining data is lost.
- Install over Wi-Fi, ideally before you travel. Keep the QR code private: anyone who scans it first can install your eSIM.
- On arrival, switch on the Roam Caller line and turn on data roaming for that line only.

**Validity.** Unless a plan says otherwise, the validity period starts when the eSIM first connects to a supported network in a destination it covers, not when you buy it. A plan ends when its validity period ends or its data runs out, whichever comes first. Unused data does not carry over and has no cash value. An eSIM that is never installed may expire after the period shown with its plan.

**Top-ups.** You can add data to a compatible eSIM before or after it runs out, while the eSIM is still on your phone. Each top-up has its own data and validity, shown before you buy.
