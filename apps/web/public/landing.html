Does your phone take an eSIM?

Most phones from the last few years do. Your phone also needs to be carrier-unlocked.

The 10-second check

Dial this on your phone. If you see an EID number, your phone supports eSIM.

What is an eSIM?

An eSIM is a SIM card built into your phone. Instead of putting in a plastic SIM, you scan a QR code and the plan downloads. You can keep your physical home SIM in at the same time.

Will I still get calls and SMS on my Nigerian (or Ghanaian, Kenyan…) number?

Yes. Leave your home SIM switched on for calls and texts, and set Roam Africa as your mobile data line. Your WhatsApp account stays on your home number. Your home network may charge for calls you receive abroad.

When does my plan start counting days?

Validity starts when the eSIM first connects to a network in your destination, not when you buy it. Buy a week early and install it at home.

Can I top up if I run out?

Yes. Top up from your account or from the link in your email. The new data lands on the same eSIM, so there is nothing to reinstall.

How do I pay from Nigeria with FX limits on my card?

Pay in Naira by bank transfer or with a local debit card. We handle the currency on our side, so your card's dollar limit doesn't come into it.

Can I get a refund?

If you haven't installed the eSIM, you can get a full refund within 30 days of purchase. If it was installed but never worked, our support team will fix it or refund you.
